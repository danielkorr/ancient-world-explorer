Alexander / Granicus River

Research dossier · example

The crossing is not the conclusion.

A dossier brings the evidence together without collapsing different kinds of knowledge into one confident sentence.

Executive synthesis

Ancient accounts place Alexander at the Granicus, but the exact crossing and battlefield geography remain matters of interpretation.

Source-supported Ancient narratives consistently describe a river crossing and battle.
Qualified Modern location proposals do not agree on every geographic detail.
Research lead Public archaeology records suggest places to inspect, not proof of association.
Open question Which physical features best fit the accounts and later landscape?

What the Lab refuses to do.

  • Turn a search result into proof.
  • Erase disagreement to make a cleaner story.
  • Present machine confidence as historical truth.
  • Hide the trail from the next reader.
